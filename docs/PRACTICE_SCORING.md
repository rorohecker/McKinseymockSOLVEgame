# Practice scoring model

These are this simulator's rules, not McKinsey's unpublished scoring rules. Scores rank attempts at the same seed and difficulty within this practice model. They do not imply a real assessment percentile.

## Sea Wolf (100 points per site)

**Treatment quality (75% of site score).** A submitted three-microbe treatment starts at 100. Each average outside its site range costs 20; a missing desired trait costs 20; each microbe with the forbidden trait costs 20. The sum of the three normalized distances from each range midpoint costs up to another 12 points (four per attribute). Scores floor at zero. Thus two treatments can satisfy every stated requirement but receive different quality scores.

**Decision quality (25% of site score).** It starts at 100 and loses:

| Decision | Deduction from decision quality |
|---|---:|
| Profile explicitly includes the forbidden trait, or explicitly avoids the desired trait | 10 or 8, respectively |
| A shown microbe assigned to a site where it cannot form a compliant trio, or rejected despite being part of a treatment within five points of that site's best | 4 each |
| A carry-over kept when unusable at its new site, or dropped despite being part of a treatment within five points of that site's best | 5 each |
| A prospect pick that leaves a weaker best available treatment than another offer, with the other rounds held fixed | Up to 6 per round, one point per three treatment-quality points of regret |
| Earlier choices prevent access to the best treatment in the original full pool | Up to 12, one per two points of lost treatment quality |
| The submitted trio is weaker than a treatment still available in the final pool | Up to 12, one per two points of avoidable treatment-quality loss |

“Usable” means the microbe can form at least one treatment satisfying all range and trait requirements with two microbes from that site's generated pool. Rejecting a merely usable but weak microbe is allowed. A missing treatment scores zero for the site. The result screen lists each deduction and shows the best treatment available from both the final and original pools.

## Redrock Study (175 points)

Correct numeric answers within the displayed tolerance receive full credit. Slightly wrong numeric answers earn declining partial credit until the error reaches the greater of four times the tolerance or 2% of the correct magnitude. Partial credit never exceeds 60% of an item's points. Blank, invalid, or farther-off answers earn zero. Wrong multiple-choice answers earn zero. Chart values receive half of their usual points if the chart type is wrong, even when the values themselves are accurate. Analysis, written report, chart, and cases retain their displayed maximum points.

## Sustainable Futures Lab (100 points)

**Project Lead:** ranking is worth 20, twelve decisions 60, consistency 10, and project outcome 10. Ranking loses points for reversed priority pairs. Decision options earn 5, 2.5, or 0. Conflicting decision approaches cost 3 consistency points per pair. Weak evidence, lost trust, and excess pressure lower the outcome score. Skipped decisions proportionally reduce consistency and outcome credit.

**Team Lab:** each day has 3 exploration, 16 assignment, 4 support, and 4 reflection raw points. Three questions earn full exploration credit only when they include both a researcher and a station. Assignment credit requires skill fit, with one additional point for the matching reason. Support choices earn 2, 1, or 0 each. Reflection compares the recorded team state with the state implied by assignments and support. The three daily totals are scaled to 100.

Focused drills keep their separate accuracy scores. New history entries are marked as scoring model 2. Prior entries retain their original scores and appear as legacy in the history table. Averages, trends, and seed comparisons use only runs on the current model and matching difficulty; replay a seed to compare under the current rules.
