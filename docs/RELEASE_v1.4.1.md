# Solve Practice Lab v1.4.1

## Interface update

- Sea Wolf traits now use mnemonic, neutral symbols for heat, pressure, phosphorus removal, nitrogen fixing, salt, acid, light sensitivity, and biofilm formation. The key stays available beside the site note.
- Site ranges and traits sit on paper-style side notes on wide screens and compact notes on narrow screens. Microbes have varied pixel silhouettes and colors that do not encode their traits or quality.
- The treatment panel no longer computes averages or labels choices as in or out of range before submission. Results still explain the outcome afterward.
- Redrock, Sea Wolf, and Sustainable Futures now use distinct pixel art backgrounds and more varied matching palettes. Background overlays are slightly lighter while information cards remain readable.
- The main menu includes seven practical tips on labelled notes, calculator history, three-microbe arithmetic, units, selection, decisions, and pacing. Links to source guidance are included. The tips do not claim an official score bonus for notes or calculator use.

## Checks

- Browser routes, unit tests, scenario generation and JavaScript/Python parity passed. Sea Wolf layout and menu tips were reviewed at a true 390-pixel mobile viewport, with no horizontal overflow.
- The Windows executable and installer passed bundled asset, desktop window, checksum, install and uninstall checks.

Download `SolvePracticeLab-Setup-1.4.1.exe` to install or `SolvePracticeLab.exe` for portable use. SHA-256 files are supplied for both. Practice scores are independent feedback, not official assessment results.
