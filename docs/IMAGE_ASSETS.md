# Generated scene assets

The following game backgrounds were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for v1.4.1. They are used behind opaque information cards, with a translucent overlay to maintain legibility.

## Redrock wetlands

File: `app/assets/redrock-wetlands-background.png`

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-bleed background for a readable desktop practice game, landscape 16:9. Primary request: an original Redrock Island environment in detailed hand-crafted 2D pixel art, with warm rust-red cliffs, ochre sandstone, pale turquoise river wetlands, sparse pines and survey boardwalks, small field research station on one side. Scene should feel like an exploration game at late morning with clear bright light, soft cream clouds, atmospheric depth, and visible pixel clusters. Palette: terracotta, amber, moss green, muted turquoise, lavender shadows. Composition: an open quiet middle area behind UI cards, environmental details concentrated near edges and lower third. Sharp pixel edges, no blur, no grid, no interface, no text, no logo, no characters, no microbe icons. Make distinct from an ocean harbor sunset background.

## Sustainable Futures campus

File: `app/assets/sustainable-campus-background.png`

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-bleed background for a readable desktop practice game, landscape 16:9. Primary request: original Sustainable Futures Lab environment in richly detailed hand-crafted 2D pixel art. Show a hopeful compact town research campus with rooftop gardens, solar arrays, small wind turbines, electric tram, community greenhouse and tree-lined paths. Bright clear afternoon under a warm pale sky, with atmospheric depth and crisp visible pixel clusters. Palette: leaf green, sunflower yellow, warm cream, brick red, lavender-blue shadows; avoid a teal/blue dominant palette. Composition: open quiet middle area behind UI cards, scene details mostly at outer edges and lower third. Sharp pixel edges, no blur, no grid, no interface, no text, no logo, no people, no microbe icons. Distinct from a desert wetland landscape and ocean harbor sunset.
