# Published datasets (all from practice platforms, none from McKinsey)

These describe **practice simulator** users, who are self-selected. They say nothing about real pass rates.

## SolvePrep "State of McKinsey Solve 2026" (pulled 20 Aug 2026, release v1.2)

| Simulator | Runs | Distinct users | Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sea Wolf | 7,152 | 3,061 | 30 Dec 2025 to 19 Aug 2026 |
| Red Rock | 11,063 | 3,528 | 22 Feb to 20 Aug 2026 |
| SFL | 8,407 | 2,415 | 1 Apr to 20 Aug 2026 |
| **Total** | 26,622 | 5,174 candidates | |

Score gain from first run to best run (users with 2+ runs):

| Game | Repeat users | Median gain | Median first to best |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red Rock | 2,043 | +20.3 pp (CI 18.9-21.6) | 61.2% to 81.5% |
| SFL | 1,486 | +10.4 pp | 58.4% to 68.8% |
| Sea Wolf | 1,214 | +6.7 pp | 71.4% to 78.1% |

After a permutation control for regression to the mean, about half the headline gain remains: Red Rock +11.4, SFL +5.7, Sea Wolf +3.3 pp. The study itself calls the headline an upper bound.

Pooled median practice scores: Red Rock 66.0%, SFL 63.4%, Sea Wolf 76.5%.

Where points are lost:
- Red Rock: written report 41.2%, analysis 38.5%, chart building 30.4%, cases 27.9% of available points.
- SFL: consistency across linked decisions 60.7%, priority ranking 39.1%, situational judgement 33.5%, stakeholder management 24.5%.
- Sea Wolf (21,456 submitted sites): average out of range 33.5%, forbidden trait present 12.6%, desired trait missing 9.8%.

Practice behaviour: 47% of game-user pairs played once and never repeated; 70% of candidates who stated a test date first practised 0-2 days before it; median lead time one day.

Stated limits: SolvePrep's own scores and weights; self-selected users; test dates self-reported; different observation windows per game.
