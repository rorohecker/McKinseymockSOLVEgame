# Scoring: what is known

## Official

McKinsey has **not** published a scoring model, a pass mark, weights, or percentile tables. It says Solve results are one input next to the rest of the application, and the recruiter decides on multiple factors. Candidates may also be asked at random to redo a task in person or explain their logic.

## Reported (not official)

| Topic | Report | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Score shown | Result emails contain no score. Candidates ask the recruiter and some get a raw score plus decile. At least eight candidates in 2026 reported this. | REPORTED |
| Raw-score range | About 499 to 558; the scale appears to top out near 560. | REPORTED |
| Raw to decile | 558 = 10th, 530 = 9th, 510 = 6th, 499 = 4th (CaseStar). One forum poster says 510 = 7th. | CONTRADICTED |
| Equating | Deciles are reportedly equated by role, region and test version. | REPORTED |
| Turnaround | 1 to 3 weeks, sometimes up to 6. | REPORTED |
| Validity | A result is reportedly valid about 12 months; retake needs a new application. | REPORTED |
| Advance rate | 20-30% (coach). | UNVERIFIED |

## Product vs process

Many providers say the game scores both **product** (what you achieved) and **process** (how you got there, from click and time telemetry). CaseStar labels this "plausible but unconfirmed". SolvePrep calls it "well-established folklore". The behaviours counted and their weights have never been published. Indicators such as time per question, journal use or revisits are vendor guesses.

## Per-game scoring in vendor models

| Game | Vendor model | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sea Wolf | 100 per site, -20 per missed requirement (see `SEA_WOLF.md`). | Several vendors agree on the basic idea; details differ (D3). |
| Redrock | About 175 points: Analysis 60, Written Review 25, Visual Report 30, Cases 60; +/-0.5 tolerance (SolvePrep). | One vendor's own weights. |
| SFL | Components weighted by the vendor (priority ranking, judgement, stakeholder management, consistency). | Vendor's own components. |

## Practical meaning

- Practice scores from any simulator, including this one, are **not** McKinsey scores. They are directional feedback.
- Because deciles are equated and the raw scale is hidden, a "good" practice score cannot be translated into a real percentile.
- A candidate who reports top-decile results described finishing one Sea Wolf site at about 80% (CaseStar, candidate-reported), so a perfect score on every site is not required.
