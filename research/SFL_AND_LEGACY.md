# Sustainable Futures Lab and retired games

## Sustainable Futures Lab (SFL)

- Appeared around March 2026 on longer invitations.
- **Standard (about 20 min):** 13 sequential questions. Question 1 is a drag-and-drop priority ranking (four actions). Questions 2-13 are scenario multiple-choice questions on one storyline; earlier answers shape later context (SolvePrep, MyConsultingCoach, CaseStar).
- **New (about 30 min, since late Aug 2026, pilot):** manage a small research team (four researchers, four workstations, three simulated days) (SolvePrep). Evidence is thin.
- No calculations or chart building.
- Reported traits tested: prioritisation, decisions under uncertainty, interpreting messy information, trade-offs, team and stakeholder effectiveness (MyConsultingCoach). VENDOR CLAIM.
- Reported question types: initial prioritisation, new conflicting data, depth of analysis, material discrepancies, team dynamics, definition of success, problem structuring, stakeholder communication, scope control, final synthesis.
- **Vendor dataset (SolvePrep, n = 8,407 practice runs):** consistency across linked decisions was the weakest component (60.7% of points lost). Components are SolvePrep's own, not McKinsey's.
- Example scenario in a coaching post: a burn-scar watershed before a storm with limited crews and materials. Stronger answers concentrate resources on the highest-consequence sites and re-prioritise when new evidence changes the risk.
- McKinsey's public page does not name SFL.

## Retired or legacy games

| Game | Notes | Label |
|---|---|---|
| **Ecosystem Building** | Choose a viable ecosystem from a pool of 39 species (3 groups of 13: 3 producers + 10 animals each); newer versions require exactly 8 species; environment e.g. aquatic, alpine, jungle; rules about calories and feeding order; then pick a location. About 30 min. Used about 2018 to 2025/26. | REPORTED (V4, V5, V22) |
| **Plant Defense** | Turn-based defence of a plant with tower-like units; replaced by Redrock (2023 per one guide). Old forum coaches quote 30-38 turns in rounds 1-2 and 42-48 in round 3 as strong. | REPORTED (V23, F5) |
| **Disaster Management** | Identify a disaster type then relocate animals to a sustainable area. | REPORTED (V24) |
| **Disease Management** | Timer-based, three periods, identify the spread pattern and predict affected animals. | REPORTED (V24) |
| **Migration Management** | Turn-based across harder maps; animals lost if resources are insufficient. | REPORTED (V24) |
| **Sustainability (reading)** | Passage with multiple-choice / true-false questions; possibly experimental. | UNVERIFIED (V24) |

### Ecosystem eating rules (as reported, V22)

1. Each species eats once. 2. A species dies if it lacks calories or its prey is gone. 3. The species providing the most calories feeds first. 4. It takes the highest-calorie prey available. 5. Calories consumed equal its need. 6. Ties split equally. 7. Repeat for the next species.

These are not implemented in this project, but the structure is simple to reproduce (see `docs/IDEAS.md`).
