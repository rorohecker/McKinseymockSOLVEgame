# Redrock Study

Time: 35 minutes, one shared clock. Practice guidance (not official): about 5 min Investigation, 12 min Analysis, 5 min Report, 12 min for six cases, 1 min spare (CaseStar). SolvePrep suggests roughly 15 / 10 / 10 across Investigation+Analysis, Report, Cases. Both are coaching estimates.

## Phases

| Phase | What happens | Notes (label) |
|---|---|---|
| Investigation | Read research objective, study text, exhibits. Save the useful numbers to a Research Journal. | Reported that only about 10-15% (MyConsultingCoach) or about half (CaseStar) of the figures matter. Investigation is not separately scored in vendor models. |
| Analysis | Calculation questions using the journal and an in-game calculator. | Usually about 3 questions with sub-parts. Compound growth, percentage points and probability named as hardest. |
| Report | Fill a short written report (dropdowns or calculated values) and choose a chart. | One-way gate: you cannot return to earlier phases. Chart choices named: bar, line, pie. |
| Cases | Six standalone mini-cases with their own small exhibits. | About 2 minutes each. Journal resets between cases. Completed cases lock. |

## Question and math types reported

1. Percent change: change divided by the **starting** value (120 to 150 is +25%, not +20%).
2. Percentage points vs percent: 14.4% to 18.0% is +3.6 points, which is +25% relative.
3. Shares of a total: build the total from all parts in the same period and unit.
4. Weighted averages: total divided by total, not the mean of group means.
5. Growth and CAGR: (end/start)^(1/years) - 1. Projecting "same rate again" differs from "same absolute increase".
6. Probability: independent events multiply; expected value = sum of probability x outcome.
7. Chart choice: line = change over time, pie/100% stack = parts of a whole, bar = category comparison, scatter = relationship, waterfall = bridge between totals.
8. Data judgement: what a table does or does not prove (rates vs counts, causation).

## Worked examples published by vendors (original exercises, not real questions)

- Silverbrook / Granite Hollow wolf-pack hunts with percent-point growth assumptions, and which chart to use (StrategyU).
- Otter shares by region: 96/640 = 15.00%, 75/410 = 18.29%, 60/520 = 11.54%; recommend by share, not by raw count (CaseStar).
- Bighorn sheep 1,500 to 1,860 = +24.00%; same rate again gives about 2,306 (CaseStar).
- Six mini-cases: growth, weighted average ticket price, independent probability, cost-effectiveness ratio, waterfall chart choice, data judgement (CaseStar).

## Scoring models published by vendors (not McKinsey's)

- SolvePrep: Analysis 6 answers x 10; written review about 25; visual report about 30 (chart type separate); cases 6 x 10; total about 175; tolerance +/-0.5.
- CaseStar: practice sittings score 27-28 items; real test reportedly about 14 scored decisions.

## Frequent mistakes (vendor data, see STATISTICS.md)

Wrong denominator or year, percent vs points, rounding intermediate results, sign errors on declines, wrong chart, spending too long in the Study and not reaching cases 5-6.
