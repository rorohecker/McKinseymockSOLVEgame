| Invite says | Games (fixed order) | Clock per game |
|---|---|---|
| about 65 min | Redrock Study, Sea Wolf | 35 + 30 |

Official position (McKinsey FAQ): the invite states the length, and each task starts with an untimed tutorial. McKinsey does not publish which games a given length contains. Time does not carry between games (reported), but within Sea Wolf the 30 minutes is shared across the three sites.

## Game-level one-liners

- **Redrock Study** (35 min): data study + 6 cases. Quantitative reasoning, chart reading, synthesis.
- **Sea Wolf** (30 min, also called Ocean Cleanup / Ocean Treatment / Microbe game): constraint filtering and averaging across 3 sites.
- **Sustainable Futures Lab** (20 or 30 min): judgement and prioritisation in a branching project scenario.

## Constructs Imbellus is said to measure (VENDOR CLAIM, V23)

Critical thinking, decision-making, metacognition, situational awareness, systems thinking. McKinsey's own blog says only that the tasks reveal how people approach problems.
