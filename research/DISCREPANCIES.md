## Reliability notes

- Several "independent" sources copy each other's structure, so agreement across vendors is weaker evidence than agreement between candidates.
- Most candidate-voice data comes from forum threads where coaches answer, and several coaches sell paid prep.
- The only dataset with a stated method for repeat-run gains also states that roughly half of its headline gain looks like noise (see `STATISTICS.md`).
